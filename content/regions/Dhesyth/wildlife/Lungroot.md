Lungroot is a plant that is only found in the region of [[Dhesyth]].  a lumenescant plant that has the rare property to purify the air around it. the people of Dhesyth put these plants in their masks to be able to stay in toxic areas. if eaten by someone who has been poisoned, it will cure them and make them feel rejuvenated.
