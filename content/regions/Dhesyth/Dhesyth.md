## description
The land of Dhesyth is uninhabitable to most. A poison filled landscape which, if you're not prepared, will kill most people.  Most of Dhesyth's landscape is a treacherous waterlogged mire, with posion emitting from the ground as well as the nature.  a notable one of these would be poisonous trees called, [[the weepers]].  apart from the mire, you have three other regions, a forest, a valley, and a mountain. while running water is fine and drinkable, stagnant water is especially dangerous, being acidic when touched.

## Early history 
As long as the land of Dhesyth has been around, the air in it's regions have been incredibly poisonous, and uninhabitable to anyone except the wildlife. In the early years of the region, the inhabitants that managed to survive, found groves in the little forests that existed with a plant called the [[Lungroot]], which is able to filter the toxins out of the air, making these places a prime spot for civilizations to blossom. some people also put the lungroot into masks so they could venture beyond their safehavens.

## The plague
As the [[Lungroot]] was harvested from the groves, villages and towns came to be in places which was normally not achievable, however, the people of Dhesyth would soon be hit with something awful. A disease known as [[Ruplexia]], emerged out of nowhere and killed thousands of inhabitants.  The disease seemed to spread like a curse, with seemingly no visible way of transference. By chance however, a desperate Druid found out, that if someone came in contact with Dhesyth's poisonous air, the symtoms of Ruplexia seemed to retract.
