## Description
The land of Dhesyth, also known as the bog is uninhabitable to most. A poison filled landscape which, if you're not prepared, will kill most people.  Most of Dhesyth's landscape is a treacherous waterlogged mire, with posion emitting from the ground as well as the nature.  A notable one of these would be poisonous trees called [[the weepers]].  Apart from the mire, you have three other regions, a forest, a valley, and a mountain. While running water is fine and drinkable, stagnant water is especially dangerous, being acidic when touched.

## Early history 
As long as the land of Dhesyth has been around, the air in it's regions have been incredibly poisonous, and uninhabitable to anyone except the wildlife. In the early years of the region, the inhabitants that managed to survive, found groves in the little forests that existed with a plant called the [[Lungroot]], which is able to filter the toxins out of the air, making these places a prime spot for civilizations to blossom. The towns made in these groves were built on elevation. Either on platforms above the swamp or on the trees itself. Some people also put the lungroot into masks so they could venture beyond their safehavens.

## The expansion
With time, the people of Dhesyth were able to harvest the [[Lungroot]] from these groves, which made the building of cities in other places in Dhesyth possible. Here is also when the culture of [[toxin masks]] was made. Herbologists and Druids started putting the flowers of the Lungroot plant into their masks, making them able to breathe the toxic air. which in turn, allowed them to travel and build towns, and eventually cities where this was not possible at first.

### Poison storms
once every three to four days, a natural event called a poison storm occurs, prompting almost everyone in Dhesyth to stay indoors. This event is Akin to a sandstorm you'd see in a desert. The poisonous air held deep underground is launched up through geysers located everywhere in Dhesyth, turning the air even more deadly than it already was. Locals can tell when this will occur based off of how every bit of wildlife will retreat, shrink or hide.
