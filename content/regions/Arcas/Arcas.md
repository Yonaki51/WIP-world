## Description
Arcas is a a region where sunny weaher covers the sky, mixed with mountains that rise from the earth and ones that drift among the clouds above, with gentle rivers flowing out of their resevoirs down the rolling hills dotted with cities, monestaries and temples. The region of Arcas thrives in border trade between other regions and cities. major cities in Arcas include: [[Hagias]], [[Theos]], [[Eriphyle]], [[Phylace]], and [[Thyrsis]].
