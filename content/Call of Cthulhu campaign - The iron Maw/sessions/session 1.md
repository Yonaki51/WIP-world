## Étienne, the woodworker

The 34 year old woodworker had been missing for 3 weeks now, but most people didn’t think that much of it, since everyone thought he just got captured by the Germans, about halfway throughout the market, he sets up his shop, and sells tiny miniature metal statues, weird abstract shapes, as well brass statues that look “very” realistic. He also moves stiffly, as well as sometimes being distracted by something that’s seemingly not there.
