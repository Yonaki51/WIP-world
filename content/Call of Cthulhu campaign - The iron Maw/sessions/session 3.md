		- Some more smaller dots scattered around

	- The players CAN attempt to head into the castle, but it is very much not advised, since it will still be swarming with Germans from the inside. if they do however, they could find:

	- If the players decide to head towards the dot on the map around the mountain, they will find a mine, that has been put out of commission because of the war. here they can find:

They can fight them at their own risk, but if they manage to defeat them, they will receive a booklet with some details regarding the ritual
		- In and around the mines, they will also find corrupted animals. some are dormant, other aggressive. options are:

			- Dogs
			- Deer
	- Some more info on the ritual needed to free this entity:
		- The ritual needs certain metallic artifacts infused with Eldritch magic, then slotted into a "gate" that resides in the castle.
