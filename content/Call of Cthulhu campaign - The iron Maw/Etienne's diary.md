some entries of note that the players can find.


## entry 1
“Went to chop wood near the treeline. Saw a deer standing between the pines. At least, I thought it was a deer. Something was off about the legs — too long, or bent wrong. It didn’t run when I noticed it. Just stared. When I blinked, it was gone. Must’ve been the light through the branches.”

## entry 2
“The Germans came through again. They asked about the old shafts. I told them the same as always — nothing down there but dust and bad air. They didn’t seem convinced.”
## entry 3
“Heard machinery from the direction of the castle last night. Strange hour for work. Sounded like metal on stone. I don’t know what they’re doing up there.”

## entry 4
“Went out to cut some firewood again at around dusk. Should’ve gone earlier, but the day got away from me. Took the path by the creek. Heard voices up ahead — thought maybe some of the boys from the village were out snaring rabbits. But it wasn’t them. Too many voices. Too low, like they weren’t meant to be heard.

I stepped closer and saw a fire in the clearing. Lanterns hanging from the branches. Four, maybe five men standing around something on the ground. A person, I think. Or an animal. Hard to tell in that light. It wasn’t lying right.

They were doing something to it. Cutting, or holding it down. It moved. One of them leaned over and I saw his hands—no, I won’t write it.

I must’ve made a sound, because they all turned at once. I ran. Took the old deer trail and doubled back by the stone wall. Lost them in the dark.

I’m home now. I think. I don’t hear anything outside.

I shouldn’t have gone out there alone.”

## Entry 5 (after transformation)
“Back home now. Work continues tomorrow. They said I may rest tonight. I don’t remember the walk back.”

## Entry 6
“No more need for the house. Leaving soon.”
